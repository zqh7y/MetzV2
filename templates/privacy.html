Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ updated }}

Metz is a small app for finding and organising meetups. This page explains exactly what it stores, why, and how to get rid of it. Google Play requires a policy like this because the app can ask for your location.

What is collected

Account Email address and a display name
Profile Optional bio and avatar emoji
Activity Meetings you create, join or are marked as attending
Location Approximate position, only while the app is open

How location is used

Location is used for one thing: drawing a marker for where you are on the map, so you can tell how far away a meeting is. It is read only while the app is open, it is never recorded on the server, and it is never attached to your account or shared with anyone. Declining the permission leaves every other part of the app working.

What other people can see

Your display name, avatar, account status and show-up rate are visible to other signed-in users, along with the meetings you have joined. Your email address is shown only to you and to administrators. Nothing in Metz is visible to people who are not signed in.

Storage and sharing

Data is held in the app's own database, and the stored records are encrypted at rest. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which receives your email address for that purpose. Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers or analytics companies — there are none in this app.

Deleting your data

Email {{ contact_email }} from the address on the account and it will be deleted, along with the meetings you created and your attendance history. There is no waiting period and no need to give a reason.

Children

Metz is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them. If you believe one exists, use the contact address above and it will be removed.
